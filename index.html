About us

Neuromeka AI Lab is an industrial AI robotics research and development team focused on bringing learning-based intelligence into real production environments.

Our mission is to make robots more capable, adaptable, and useful in the real world. We believe the next generation of automation will move beyond rigid, pre-programmed workflows toward robots that can perceive, learn, and safely interact with complex industrial settings, working alongside humans to expand what automation can achieve.

We develop field-ready AI for industrial robots, starting from concrete manufacturing problems and scaling toward more general robotic capabilities. Our work integrates learning-based manipulation, multi-modal perception, and safety-aware control with classical robotics systems, enabling robots to tackle challenging tasks such as deformable object manipulation and precision assembly.

By grounding advanced AI in practical constraints - safety, reliability, and scalability - we bridge cutting-edge research with production-grade deployment, laying the foundations for the next generation of intelligent industrial robots.
